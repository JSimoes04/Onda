    Reading the sea

    Three numbers decide whether a session is worth the drive. Vaga combines them into one rating, from Flat to Epic.

    1. Height

      How tall the waves are out at sea, in metres. Under half a metre the sea is flat. Past three metres, most beaches are for experts only.

    2. Period

      The seconds between one crest and the next. A long period means the swell travelled far, so its waves arrive tidy and carry more power.

    3. Wind

      Offshore wind blows from the land and holds the face of the wave up. Onshore wind blows from the sea and crumbles it. No wind at all is called glassy.
